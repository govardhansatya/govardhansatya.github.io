Research & recognition

accepted · ACM AI Summit 2026

The Adaptation–Embodied Carbon Paradox

First-authored paper (Visionary Track, presented as a poster). Argues that on-device continual learning wears out non-volatile memory and raises embodied carbon, then proposes SC-LACL, a lifetime-aware controller. In simulation it cut NVM writes 7–25× while keeping accuracy within 0.5–1.5 points of six baselines on three drift streams. Results are simulated, not hardware-tested.
